MASTER GUI DESIGN PROMPT
ALLYUS INTELLIGENCE OS — “THE LIVING BUSINESS COMMAND CENTRE”

Design a premium, emotionally reassuring, ultra-modern SaaS interface for ALLYUS, an AI Business Operating System that acts as a competent AI employee, guardian, strategist, and operational partner for small and medium businesses.

The interface must immediately make the owner feel:

“My business is under control.”
“ALLY has been working while I was away.”
“I am not alone.”
“Nothing important is being missed.”
“My AI employee is becoming more capable every day.”

The design must combine:

the emerald intelligence aesthetic and Meta-Brain visual from the supplied concept images,
the structured clarity of the current ALLYUS dashboard,
the warmth and simplicity of Apple,
the calm competence of JARVIS,
the trust and precision of enterprise software,
and the emotional reassurance of a trusted colleague.
1. OVERALL VISUAL LANGUAGE

Use a premium dark interface with:

deep charcoal and near-black background,
soft emerald gradients,
subtle glass panels,
thin luminous borders,
restrained depth and shadows,
clean white typography,
muted grey secondary text,
rounded corners,
generous spacing,
clear information hierarchy.

Use emerald green only for:

healthy status,
learning,
successful actions,
active intelligence,
positive business growth,
trusted confirmations.

Use amber for:

owner attention needed,
uncertainty,
pending approval.

Use red only for:

genuine urgent risk,
financial danger,
security breach,
critical failure.

Avoid:

excessive neon,
cluttered HUD effects,
fake sci-fi labels,
tiny unreadable charts,
meaningless statistics,
dense technical language,
too many glowing elements.

The interface must feel alive but never noisy.
